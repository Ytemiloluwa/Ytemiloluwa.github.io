Frequently Asked Questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

All subscriptions are handled through your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel your subscription by going to the Settings app on your device, tapping your name, and selecting "Subscriptions".

Are my worries private?

Yes. Your entries are stored securely on your device, and we do not have access to read your journal entries or worries.

How do I request a refund?

Because payments are processed by Apple, we cannot issue refunds directly. Please contact Apple Support or visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request a refund.
